Context

Helping mothers break into tech

TechMoms is a nonprofit that provides accessible, community-driven tech education to mothers.

The Adobe Social Impact Challenge asked teams to use Adobe tools to make a measurable difference for a nonprofit. We set out to redesign TechMoms marketing strategy.

Problems

Strong impact, limited reach

Despite a powerful mission, their outreach struggled to reach the women who needed it most — the marketing lacked clarity, urgency, and visual consistency. The challenges were clear:

The Challenge

"How might we increase awareness of TechMoms' programs and drive meaningful enrollment across diverse, underserved communities?"

Strategy Pillars

Four ways to grow

01

Market Programs & Alumni

Leverage alumni success stories and testimonials to make the value proposition tangible and emotionally compelling.

02

Expand Target Audiences

Reach underserved communities — including Latinas and women of color — with targeted programs and bilingual materials.

03

Improve Website Effectiveness

Redesign the site to be more inviting, clarify calls-to-action, separate program applications, and reduce repetition.

04

Partner with Non-profits

Collaborate with organizations like The Road Home, YWCA, and the Rape Recovery Center to bring TechMoms directly to women who need a fresh start — meeting them where they are and lowering the barrier to entry.

Takeaways

What I learned.

01

Research first, design second: The most effective marketing decisions came from understanding TechMoms' existing alumni and their stories — data and testimonials shaped every design choice.

02

Inclusive design is intentional: Reaching Latina and underserved communities required more than translation — it meant rethinking channels, tone, imagery, and program structure entirely.

03

A live prototype wins arguments: Building the actual Adobe Express website and coming in with detailed social media let judges see the vision in our actions and gave our pitch undeniable credibility.
