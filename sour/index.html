Context

Fast fashion is a problem.
Secondhand is the answer.

Fast fashion contributes nearly 10% of global carbon emissions, with millions of tons of textiles discarded every year. SOUR is a social eCommerce platform that connects eco-conscious buyers and sellers in a vibrant secondhand marketplace — reducing waste while making sustainable fashion feel effortless and fun.

Existing platforms like Depop and Poshmark charge high transaction fees and offer limited personalization. Users don't just buy clothes — they buy into a person's aesthetic. On SOUR, sellers build a visual identity through photos and short-form video, letting users connect with each others' style before they ever check a price tag. That personal connection is what turns a one-time purchase into a loyal following.

The Mission

"Make sustainable fashion more accessible, engaging, and community-driven — without the transaction fees that eat into sellers' profits."

What Makes SOUR Different

Built to stand out

01

No Transaction Fees

Sellers pay a flat $18/month subscription — keeping more of their earnings vs. Depop, Poshmark, and eBay which all take a cut of every sale.

02

AI-Generated Listings

Upload a photo and SOUR auto generates the description, tags, color info, and categorization — reducing the friction of creating a listing.

03

Video-First Social Commerce

Short-form video posts let sellers showcase items in a way static photos can't — building trust, community, and engagement simultaneously.

04

Personalized Curated Feed

A recommendation algorithm tailored to each buyer's style makes discovering secondhand pieces feel like scrolling your favorite social app.

Takeaways

What I learned

01

Build with users, not for them: Every stage of SOUR is validated with real user input — from pricing research using Van Westendorp's model to usability studies planned before final release.

02

The business model is the product: Removing transaction fees wasn't just a feature — it was the core differentiation that made SOUR's pitch compelling to investors and users alike.

03

Pitching is a skill: Presenting SOUR to investors, judges, and stakeholders repeatedly taught me how to distill a complex product into a clear, compelling story.
